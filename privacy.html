Privacy

Optional, consent-based analytics.

The project works without Analytics. Google Analytics loads only after you select “Allow analytics”.

What is measured

When allowed on the deployed site, standard page-view measurement is sent to the GA4 Web stream identified by G-PZEC365PCE. Advertising storage, Google signals, and advertising personalisation are disabled in the site configuration.

Google documents that its standard implementation can process user and session counts, approximate location, and browser and device information, and can use a first-party _ga client-ID cookie.

Your choice

Before a choice is made, Analytics storage is denied and the external Google Analytics script is not requested. Rejecting keeps the script unloaded. Allowing loads it and stores the choice in this browser under agent-workflow-blueprint.analytics-consent.v1.

Use the persistent Analytics settings control to change or withdraw your choice. Withdrawal disables further measurement on the page and removes accessible _ga cookies for this host. Reloading the page keeps Analytics unloaded.

Review boundaries

The repository verifies its loading and consent behaviour locally. It does not verify Google’s property-level retention settings, downstream processing, legal suitability for a particular visitor or organisation, or deletion of information already transmitted before consent was withdrawn.

Review Google’s Analytics data-collection documentation and privacy policy for Google’s own practices.
