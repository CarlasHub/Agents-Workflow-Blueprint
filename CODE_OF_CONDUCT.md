# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our standards

Positive behaviour includes empathy, respectful feedback, responsibility for mistakes, and focus on what is best for the community. Unacceptable behaviour includes harassment, sexualised attention, trolling, insults, threats, deliberate intimidation, and publishing another person's private information without explicit permission.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the repository owner using the non-public contact method on their GitHub profile. The maintainer may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that violate this code and may temporarily or permanently exclude contributors.
